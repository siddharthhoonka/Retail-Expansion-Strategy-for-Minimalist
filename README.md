# Retail-Expansion-Strategy-for-Minimalist

## Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze **120,000+ online skincare transactions** for **Minimalist**, one of India's leading D2C skincare brands. Using **SQL, Python, and Power BI**, this project identifies the most suitable Indian cities for offline retail expansion by analyzing customer purchasing behavior, sales performance, market demand, and financial feasibility.

The recommendations are driven by **business intelligence, customer analytics, market potential analysis, and profitability metrics** to support strategic expansion decisions.

---

# Key Business Questions Solved

### Total Revenue Analysis
Calculate the total revenue generated across all cities and product categories.

### Revenue by City
Identify the highest revenue-generating cities to prioritize offline expansion.

### Monthly Sales Trend
Analyze month-over-month sales growth to identify seasonal trends and business performance.

### Product Category Performance
Determine the best-performing skincare categories based on revenue and sales volume.

### Top Selling Products
Identify the highest-selling skincare products across India and within each city.

### Average Order Value (AOV)
Calculate the average customer spending per order for each city.

### Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)
Estimate the lifetime value of customers to identify high-value customer segments.

### Repeat Purchase Analysis
Measure customer retention by identifying cities with the highest repeat purchase rates.

### Customer Segmentation (RFM)
Segment customers into High-, Medium-, and Low-value groups using **Recency, Frequency, and Monetary (RFM)** analysis.

### Top Selling Products by City
Use **DENSE_RANK()** and **PARTITION BY** to identify the top three products in every city.

### Revenue by Product Category
Compare product category performance across different cities.

### Revenue vs. Rent Analysis
Evaluate city-wise revenue against operational costs using the Rent Index.

### Monthly Sales Growth
Use **LAG()** and window functions to calculate month-over-month sales growth by city.

### Market Potential Analysis

Perform a multi-factor analysis of every city based on:

- Total Revenue
- Total Customers
- Average Order Value
- Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)
- Population
- Average Income
- Competition Score
- Rent Index
- Revenue per Customer
- Estimated Return on Investment (ROI)

### Expansion Recommendation
Rank Indian cities based on overall market potential and recommend the best locations for Minimalist's offline retail expansion.

---

# Final Recommendation

Based on comprehensive business analysis, the following cities are recommended for the first phase of offline expansion:

### Bengaluru
- Highest total revenue
- Highest Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)
- Strong customer density
- Highest projected ROI

### Hyderabad
- High repeat purchase rate
- Strong demand growth
- Moderate competition and operational costs

### Pune
- High Average Order Value (AOV)
- Strong revenue growth
- Excellent market potential with lower expansion costs

### Ahmedabad *(Phase II Expansion)*
- Growing customer base
- Low rent index
- High profitability potential

### Jaipur *(Phase II Expansion)*
- Emerging skincare market
- Lower competition
- Cost-effective expansion opportunity
